# Decisions

A short log of what we have settled and what is still open. Keep new entries at
the top of each section.

## Accepted

These are settled, with the reasoning, so we do not relitigate them.

- **Brand hierarchy: Physical AI Systems (course/field), book title unchanged,
  TinyAgents Kit (Arduino product), dual-brain as technical signature.** See
  `BRAND.md`. “Agent” means a complete engineered system under resource and
  physical constraints, not an LLM wrapper. The kit continues the Tiny*
  education line after TinyML without renaming TinyML 2.0.
- **One public course = one curriculum spine** (`COURSE.md`). Syllabus, 14-week
  project schedule, milestones, and kit path are the same for ETH (or similar)
  enrollment, open self-followers, and Arduino/partner packaging. Do not
  maintain a separate “material development curriculum” or ETH-only syllabus.
  `TEACHING-FLOW.md` / `CHAPTER-OUTLINES.md` are authoring depth under that
  course, not a second offer.
  Optional lecture+lab alternate: `SEMESTER-PACK.md`.
- **Dual-brain (MPU proposes, MCU permits) is the reference teaching
  architecture for the TinyAgents Kit on Arduino UNO Q.** Concepts stay
  platform-neutral; the board makes proposal vs independent physical permission
  tangible. Not a claim of certified functional safety.
- **The canonical public offer is `COURSE.md`.** Canonical authoring sources are
  `BOOK-GOAL.md`, `CHAPTER-OUTLINES.md`, `TEACHING-FLOW.md`, `AUTHORING-SYSTEM.md`,
  `PRODUCTION-PLAN.md`, `TRACEABILITY.md`, and `BRAND.md`. `WAVE-1-BRIEFS.md` is
  the accepted working packet for Chapters 1 through 4. Older book plans remain
  as decision history but no longer control drafting.
- **The backward-design machinery stays out of the visible chapter hierarchy.**
  The learning objective is a compact callout, not a section. Opening question,
  entering state, misconception, crux, and chapter dependency remain editorial
  metadata. The decision callout records the dossier update, and the lab is the
  final reader-facing element.
- **The scope test begins with a learned-component premise, then requires
  consequential physical feedback and delegated physical authority.** Feedback
  by itself does not distinguish this subject from digital or fixed-control
  systems that alter their future inputs. This test is the book's working
  synthesis rather than a claimed consensus definition.
- **The canonical case is a platform-neutral shared-workspace handling
  system.** It supports simple and modern learned components, different
  embodiments, human authority, interaction data, and end-to-end evidence
  without defining the book around one product.
- **Context packet `CTX-FND-001` is accepted for research and chapter
  architecture.** It now authorizes a progressive Chapter 1 opening-cluster
  pilot and provisional asset production. Full-chapter acceptance still
  requires built and verified visual assets, bibliography cleanup, and a final
  red-team check of the prose and figures.
- **The book has thirteen teaching chapters and an unnumbered final design
  review.** Each chapter adds one capability and dossier artifact. The design
  review integrates them without pretending that synthesis is a new topic.
- **Human authority precedes learning from interaction, and the release
  decision comes last.** Permission to observe, retain, teach, and change must
  exist before interaction becomes training data. The deployment decision
  integrates both authority and governed change.
- **The concept track is complete before every lab.** Analytical, hosted, and
  physical manifestations assess the same phenomenon, evidence, failure, and
  decision. Hardware never repairs missing pedagogy.
- **The core manuscript is model- and platform-neutral.** VLMs, VLAs, boards,
  robots, and hosted tools are case studies or lab realizations, not the
  organizing spine.
- **The visual language follows the two-world-state grammar.** Learned proposals
  remain visually distinct from allowed commands, and human authority attaches
  to specific operations rather than appearing as a generic person icon.
- **The title is *Physical AI: Machine Learning Systems That Sense and Act*.**
  The title keeps the recognized field name while the subtitle makes the
  progression from machine learning systems explicit.

Earlier prototype decisions are preserved separately in
`LEGACY-PROTOTYPE-DECISIONS.md`. They do not govern this book.
